## Critical Issues (2)

### C1 — Doubled caption label (all 5 tables + List of Tables)
**File:** `config/settings.tex` (caption setup) + `chapter1.tex` (all 5 captions)
**Found by:** latex-figure-specialist
**Detail:** `\captionsetup` uses `labelfont=bf, labelsep=period`, so LaTeX generates "**Table 1.1.**" automatically, but each caption text also begins with "Table 1.1 --- ". The rendered output is: **Table 1.1.** Table 1.1 --- Evolution of...
Affects all 5 tables in Chapter 1, and the List of Tables. Systemic, jury-visible.
**Fix:** Add to `config/settings.tex`:
```latex
\DeclareCaptionLabelFormat{thesisdash}{#1 #2 ---}
\captionsetup[table]{labelformat=thesisdash, labelsep=space, labelfont=bf}
```
Then strip the "Table N.N --- " prefix from all five `\caption{}` texts.

### C2 — C1 dimension definitions buried in prose (lines 137, 144)
**File:** `chapter1.tex:137` and `chapter1.tex:144`
**Found by:** writing-reviewer AND logic-reviewer (independently)
**Detail:** §1.5 defines 5 structural dimensions (line 137) and 5 analytical dimensions (line 144) as dense prose paragraphs with inline `\textit{}`. These are term-definition pairs — textbook `description` list material. Since these ten dimensions are the chapter's payload (C1) and are cross-referenced in Chapters 2 and 3, they must be individually scannable.
**Fix:** Convert both to `\begin{description}...\end{description}` lists. Keep the "Together these five dimensions..." closing sentence after each list. The two parallel description lists also reinforce the structural/analytical symmetry visually.
